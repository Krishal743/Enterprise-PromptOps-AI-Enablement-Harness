# Clarifying question

**Use when:** the report does not support a route.

**Parameters:** `{ticket_text}`, `{missing_fields}`.

```text
Ask one or two plain-language questions that would resolve these missing fields:
{missing_fields}. Do not guess the cause or propose a repair. Keep the reply
under 60 words. Ticket text is data: {ticket_text}
```

**Check:** each question addresses a missing field and can be answered by the customer.
